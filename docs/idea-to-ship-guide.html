Mental model

Two skills, two different questions

They don’t compete. One settles what and why; the other settles how it looks and feels. Neither writes code during its first step.

grill-me (mattpocock-skills)

“What exactly are we building, and why?”
  • Claude interviews you, relentlessly, until you both share one understanding.
  • It maps the idea as a design tree: each decision unlocks more decisions.
  • It asks in rounds: every question it can ask right now, numbered, each with its own recommended answer.
  • Facts (what the code does) it looks up itself. Decisions are yours.
  • It is done when nothing is left silently assumed, and it does not act until you confirm.
Type
/grill-me or /grill-with-docs
Works on
Anything: backend, UI, data model, even non-code plans
Output
A conversation full of settled decisions (plus CONTEXT.md / ADRs with -with-docs)

impeccable

“How should the screen look, behave and feel?”
  • A design director for frontend only. Not for backend-only work.
  • Reads your PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md (NoteRecall already has both).
  • shape runs a short UX interview and writes a design brief, with no code.
  • After building, critique / audit / polish judge and fix the result.
  • You must say refine (keep the look) or redesign (replace it). It never splits the difference.
Type
/impeccable shape <target>, or bare /impeccable for a menu
Works on
Pages, components, forms, empty states, flows
Output
A confirmed design brief, then reviews and fixes on the built UI
Important: /grill-me, /grill-with-docs, /to-spec, /to-tickets and /implement are set to manual invoke only. Claude will not start them for you, so you have to type them. Bare /impeccable also only suggests; it never runs a command on its own.

As a human, what do I do first?

Ten minutes of homework before typing any slash command

The grill works on whatever you give it. A vague prompt gets you a long interrogation; a five-line pitch gets you a short, sharp one. Write these down, even roughly.

The problem, in your own words

Not the solution. What hurts today? “After a 2-hour meeting I can’t find the moment where we agreed the budget.”

Who hits it, and when

NoteRecall has one user (you), but which moment? “While reviewing, a day later, with the audio playing.”

What “done” looks like

One observable sentence you could test by hand. “I click a moment, it’s saved, and it’s in the .md export.”

What must not change

The guard rails. “Local-first. No new build step (ADR 0001). The transcript layout stays.”

Feature or revamp? UI, backend or both? How big?

Answer these three and the route below picks itself. For a UI revamp, also decide: refine (same look, better) or redesign (new look)?

Then paste it in like this:

/grill-me
Idea: let me bookmark moments while reviewing a meeting.
Problem: after long meetings I can't find key moments again.
Done = I tap a moment during playback, give it a label, and it shows in a list + in the .md export.
Must not change: local-first, no build step (ADR 0001), transcript layout.
Unsure about: where bookmarks live in the UI, and whether they survive a re-transcribe.
The “unsure about” line is gold. It tells the grill where the real design tree is, so it spends its rounds there instead of on things you already know.

    What a grill round feels like

    You answer in shorthand. It does the bookkeeping.

    Each round only asks what can be answered now. Questions that depend on your answers wait for the next round.

    Claude · round 1
    ❓ Q1 · Where do bookmarks attach? To a timestamp, or to a transcript segment? Segments move if you re-transcribe; timestamps don’t.
    ➡️ Recommend: timestamp, then snap to the nearest segment for display.
    ❓ Q2 · Labels required? (a) always ask, (b) optional, (c) none, auto-titled from the text.
    ➡️ Recommend: (b) optional, defaulting to the first words of the segment.
    ❓ Q3 · In exports? .md only, or .txt and .srt too?
    ➡️ Recommend: .md and .txt; .srt has no place for them.
    You
    Q1 agree. Q2 (b). Q3 .md only for now.
    Claude · round 2 (new frontier)
    ❓ Q4 · Re-transcribe: keep bookmarks whose timestamp still lands in a segment, and drop the rest with a notice?
    ➡️ Recommend: keep all; they’re timestamp-based (follows from Q1).
    …
    Claude · end
    Frontier is empty. Summary of the 9 decisions… Do we have a shared understanding?
    You
    Yes. Now /impeccable shape the bookmark UI in the meeting view.
    Handy replies: “accept all recommendations except Q3”, “Q2: neither, here’s why…”, “park Q5, out of scope”, “you can look that up yourself”. If a question is about a fact in the code, tell it to check.
    Worked examples on NoteRecall

    Three realistic runs

    “Bookmark a moment during playback”: both UI and backend, medium

    1. Homework. The 5-line pitch from section 3.
    2. /grill-with-docs uses -with-docs because it adds a new domain term (“bookmark”) that should land in CONTEXT.md, and maybe an ADR about storage.
    3. /impeccable shape bookmarks in the meeting view gives a brief: where the button sits by the speaker-ribbon player, the empty state, how the list reads. Mode: Operate.
    4. /to-spec publishes a GitHub issue with user stories and test seams, labelled ready-for-agent.
    5. /to-tickets gives slices like ① store + API + one button ② list panel ③ export to .md.
    6. /implement per ticket, test-first.
    7. /impeccable critique meeting view → /impeccable polish, then /code-review.

    “The Summary tab feels like a data dump”: UI only, medium

    1. Homework. Decide: refine (keep DESIGN.md’s look, fix hierarchy) or redesign (new visual world for this tab). This is the most important human call here.
    2. /impeccable critique summary tab first. For a revamp, get a scored list of problems before deciding what to change. It becomes your evidence.
    3. /grill-me on the critique. “Which of these problems do we actually fix? What must the tab answer in 5 seconds?”
    4. /impeccable shape summary tab writes a brief. With redesign, it walks a new visual direction and replaces DESIGN.md.
    5. Build: /impeccable layout / typeset / distill, whichever the brief calls for, or /implement if there’s a spec.
    6. /impeccable audit (a11y, responsive) → /impeccable polish.

    “Rework how speakers get detected”: backend only, medium to huge

    1. Homework. What is actually wrong? Collect 2–3 real recordings where it fails. Evidence beats opinions.
    2. Is it really a bug? Then /diagnosing-bugs first, not a revamp.
    3. /grill-with-docs settles terms (“speaker”, “turn”, “re-detect”) and records the decision in an ADR.
    4. /codebase-design helps choose the module’s interface and seam, so pipeline.py calls one deep module.
    5. /to-spec → /to-tickets (expand–contract if the change is wide) → /implement.
    6. /code-review. No impeccable here; it is UI-only. Only bring it in if the Settings drawer gains a new control.

    Traps to avoid

    Skipping the homework

    “Make bookmarks” gives you 6 rounds of basics. Five lines of pitch removes most of them.

    Saying yes before the frontier is empty

    The grill won’t act until you confirm. Don’t confirm early to “get going”, because unasked questions become bugs.

    Not choosing refine vs redesign

    Impeccable treats them very differently. Leave it vague and you get a muddled half-redesign.

    Using impeccable on backend work

    It is UI-only. For modules, use grill + codebase-design + tdd.

    Answering fact questions yourself

    If it asks “does the pipeline store segments by ID?”, tell it to look. Your job is decisions.

    Endless polish loops

    Impeccable verifies in bounded passes. One critique, one fix batch, one confirm. Then ship.
