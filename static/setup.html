Transcription engine

💻 Local engine settings

Only NVIDIA GPUs accelerate transcription — AMD/Intel graphics fall back to CPU automatically.
Whisper model:
💡 Accuracy comes from the model, not the device. CPU runs the same weights as GPU and produces the same text — only slower. It is the small models that cause errors, and on bahasa rojak they don't merely mishear: they silently translate the English into Malay, which reads fluently and is simply not what was said.
Measured on 95 s of real meeting audio, CPU-only, for the line “I'm the leader for the infrastructure”:
base 5.5× realtime “I'm Charles from Y&E… doing a four on the infrastructure”
medium 3.7× realtime “saya akan menulis tentang impran” — translated, not transcribed
large-v3 2.5× realtime “By the way, I'm Syarul from IT. So, I'm the leader for the infrastructure.” ✓
large-v3 is only ~2× slower than base — roughly 24 min for a 1-hour meeting on a 20-core CPU, with no GPU. base and small were removed from this list: they produce wrong data, which is worse than waiting.

☁️ Google Gemini settings

⚠️ Cloud mode uploads your recordings to Google's servers (kept ~48 h). Don't use it for confidential meetings. Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey.
Audio is sent in ~5 min chunks. Gemini is asked for the words only — timing and speaker labels are computed on this machine.
