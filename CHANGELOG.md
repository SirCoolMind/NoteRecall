# Changelog

What changed in each version of NoteRecall, newest first. To update, open
**Settings (gear icon) → Updates**.

## 0.1.0

The first numbered version.

- Turn a meeting recording into a transcript that says who spoke, in Bahasa
  Melayu, English or both mixed together, plus a short summary.
- Runs on your own computer. Recordings stay private unless you choose the
  optional Google Gemini engine.
- Rename speakers, jump the audio to any line, and re-detect speakers with a
  fixed number of people.
- Download as `.txt`, `.md` (with summary) or `.srt` subtitles.
- Set up everything from inside the app, with progress for each item.
- Malay and English screens, light and dark themes.
- Update NoteRecall from inside the app (Settings → Updates). Your meetings,
  settings and downloaded models are kept.
