# Changelog

What changed in each version of NoteRecall, newest first. To update, open
**Settings (gear icon) → Updates**.

## Unreleased

Not in a numbered version yet.

- **Correct a transcript.** Fix a line's text in place, give a line or a whole
  turn to another speaker, delete a line, and merge two speakers into one.
  The transcript as it was first written is kept, so you can put one line back
  or discard all your corrections. Corrections show up in the downloads, and
  the built-in summary is refreshed.
- **Search everything said.** The search box on the schedule now also looks
  inside every transcript. Each result opens the meeting at that moment.
- **Listen faster.** Playback speed from 0.75× to 2× (remembered), a button to
  go back 5 seconds, and keys that work anywhere on a meeting page: Space
  plays or pauses, the arrow keys move 5 seconds, `[` and `]` change the
  speed, `/` goes to search, `?` shows the list.
- **Read while it works.** With the local engine, the transcript appears line
  by line while the recording is still being processed. The browser tab shows
  the percentage.
- Names you have already given to speakers are suggested when you rename a
  speaker in another meeting.
- Fixed: a few words could be shown as lasting several minutes, which painted
  the speaker ribbon wrongly and inflated that speaker's talk time.
- Fixed: a stray scrollbar on the Transcript / Summary tabs, and the Back
  button overlapping the language switch on a phone.

## 0.1.0

The first numbered version.

- Turn a meeting recording into a transcript that says who spoke, in Bahasa
  Melayu, English or both mixed together, plus a short summary.
- Runs on your own computer. Recordings stay private unless you choose the
  optional Google Gemini engine.
- Rename speakers, jump the audio to any line, and re-detect speakers with a
  fixed number of people.
- Download as `.txt`, `.md` (with summary) or `.srt` subtitles.
- Set up everything from inside the app, with progress for each item.
- Malay and English screens, light and dark themes.
- Update NoteRecall from inside the app (Settings → Updates). Your meetings,
  settings and downloaded models are kept.
